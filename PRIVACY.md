# Privacy Policy - Inq PDF Editor

**Last Updated:** September 10, 2026

Welcome to **Inq PDF Editor**. Your privacy is our highest architectural priority. We believe productivity tools should respect user confidentiality by design.

---

## 1. 🛡️ Client-Side Document Processing (Zero Server Transmission)

- **100% In-Browser Execution**: All document rendering, text glyph extraction, in-place text modification, redaction whiteout generation, stamp placement, and vector PDF compilation occur entirely inside your web browser using WebAssembly and client-side JavaScript (`pdf-lib` and `PDF.js`).
- **Zero Document Storage**: We do **not** upload, collect, inspect, store, or transmit your PDF files, document text, financial figures, or confidential records to any external server or third-party cloud.
- **Offline Capable**: You can disconnect your internet connection after loading the application and continue to edit and export vector PDFs seamlessly.

---

## 2. 📊 Analytics & Telemetry (Google Analytics 4)

To understand aggregate platform usage (such as total page visits, popular screen sizes, and error occurrences) and improve the software:
- We may use **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)**.
- **Google Consent Mode v2**: By default, all non-essential analytics and tracking storage are set to **denied**.
- **No Document Content in Analytics**: Custom telemetry events only track anonymous interface actions (e.g. `pdf_loaded`, `pdf_exported`). Document contents, file names, text edits, and personal data are strictly excluded.
- **IP Anonymization**: IP addresses are processed in aggregate and not persistently stored.

---

## 3. 🍪 Cookies & Local Storage

Inq PDF Editor uses minimal browser local storage:
1. `inq_theme`: Remembers your choice of Light Mode (Google Labs) or Dark Mode (Gemini).
2. `inq_cookie_consent`: Remembers your cookie preference (`accepted` or `declined`).
3. Google Analytics cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_*`): Stored **only** if you explicitly grant cookie consent.

You can reset or remove these cookies at any time via your browser settings or by clearing your site data.

---

## 4. ⚖️ Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliance)

Under international data protection regulations (including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation and California Consumer Privacy Act):
- **Right to Opt-Out**: You have the right to decline all non-essential analytics tracking at any time using the Cookie Consent banner or in-app Privacy Settings.
- **Right to Access & Deletion**: Because we never store your documents or identifiable personal data on any server, there are no remote user records or documents to delete.
- **No Sale of Personal Information**: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data or document contents under any circumstances.
